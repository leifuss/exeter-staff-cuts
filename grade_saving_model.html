Where the saving comes from — grade by grade

One grade at a time. For each of Exeter’s three main academic grades, this shows what a newly appointed member of staff is paid in each year they spend in that grade, at Exeter and at a comparator university — and what the difference is worth once new appointments have worked through. Every assumption is editable. All universities sit on the same national pay spine, so a given spine point pays the same everywhere: only the structure differs — where the grade starts, how many increments it has, and what sits above its ceiling.

Staff numbers are Exeter’s actual 2023 distribution across these spine points. Overwrite them to test any population.
