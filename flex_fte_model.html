A voluntary lever: incentivised part-time (0.8 FTE), with the tax wedge doing the work

Academic pay is hard to cut directly. But a large share of every pound above the higher-rate threshold — and an even larger share above £100k — is lost to tax, so giving up a slice of gross salary costs far less in take-home than it saves the university (which also sheds employer NI and pension). This tool prices one idea: staff voluntarily move to part-time in exchange for protected progression. It works best for higher-paid professors, but can be offered to all on fairness grounds. Illustrative and strictly voluntary — it prices a mechanism, it does not propose that anyone be pushed part-time.
