A voluntary lever: incentivised part-time (0.8 FTE) for higher-paid professors

Professorial pay is hard to change directly — it already rises below inflation, so Exeter professors tend to lose ground in real terms year on year. But at £100k+ a large share of each pound is lost to tax, so giving up a slice of gross salary costs far less in take-home than it saves the university (which also sheds employer NI and pension). This tool models one idea: professors voluntarily move to part-time in exchange for a guaranteed annual increment (up to the top of their current band). Illustrative and strictly voluntary — it prices a mechanism, it does not propose that anyone be pushed part-time.
